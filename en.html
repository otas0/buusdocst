Product

What the product does

buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool (a separate product in the Buusdo line) checks an address with our own threat list, structure signals, brand lookalikes, DNS and reachability — before you trust the site. URLs are not sent to third-party reputation services.

Halt

If you see Halt

The full Halt result (leave, verify, optional report) lives on the Workers checker. This page mirrors the same next steps when Cloudflare is reachable.

  1. Leave the page now — do not continue on the address you checked.
  2. Do not enter passwords, card numbers, or SMS codes on that site.
  3. Verify independently on an official domain or app you already trust (bookmark, bank card, known store). Do not use phone numbers or emails from the suspect page.
  4. Optional: save a local report draft on the full checker; block the host in the extension or desktop app.

Honest limits: cannot wipe data a site already collected; not antivirus; results come from the curated list and address signals — not a public vote count.
