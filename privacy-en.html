Privacy policy summary

The full privacy policy lives on the Workers site. This page is a short summary for visitors who land on the China GitHub Pages entry: no cloud account sync; block/trust lists stay on-device and can be moved with JSON/QR or desktop shield-backup.json.

Open full privacy page Back to entry

What a check uses

The address you enter is parsed on the buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool server, matched against our own threat list, and may get a reachability probe (DNS / HTTP). We do not send URLs to third-party reputation services, and we do not scrape full page content.

What is stored

Default: nothing. Browser / extension / desktop can optionally keep on-device block/trust lists or check history. There is no cloud account sync; move lists yourself with JSON/QR or desktop shield-backup.json.

Contact

Privacy questions go to the operator of this deployment. Do not paste sensitive check results in public channels.

Halt — short FAQ

Same honest limits as the entry page “If you see Halt” steps — useful before you trust a Halt result.

  • What first after Halt? Leave that page now; do not enter passwords, card numbers, or SMS codes; verify on an official domain or app you already trust.
  • Can you wipe data a site already collected? No. If you already typed secrets, change them on the real official site and watch bank / email alerts.
  • Is this antivirus? No. Closing the tab only stops you from entering more data on that address; it does not scan or clean the device.
  • Is the result a public vote? No. It comes from buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool’s own threat list and address signals — not a crowd report count.
